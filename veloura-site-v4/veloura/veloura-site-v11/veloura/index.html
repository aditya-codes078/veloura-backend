    Craft

    Four ingredients.
    Nowhere to hide.

    01

    Real fruit, real nuts, real pods

    Ratnagiri Alphonso pulped in April, Bronte pistachios roasted in-house, Madagascar bourbon pods split and scraped the morning of each churn. Nothing arrives as a bottle labelled "natural flavour".

    02

    Single-herd Jersey cream

    42% butterfat from a family dairy an hour outside Kanpur. High fat means fewer ice crystals, which is the whole trick — silk instead of frost, without a single stabiliser.

    03

    Two freezers, two philosophies

    Scooped ice cream churns at −6°C with 24% overrun. Kulfi is not churned at all: milk reduced four hours, poured into matka moulds and set in −22°C brine. Same counter, opposite methods.

    04

    Cones and bowls pressed to order

    Batter of butter, cane sugar and flaked sea salt, pressed on cast-iron waffle plates and rolled — or domed into a bowl — while pliable. Twelve seconds of warmth is the difference between crisp and sad.
